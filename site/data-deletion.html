Data Deletion

Last updated: 26 November 2026 Atharia AGI · Rangga Prayoga Hermawan

How to request deletion of any data held by neugi.com or by the Rangga.P.H-pink Meta app. Requests are processed within 7 days and confirmed by email.

1. What data we may hold

The site and the app hold very little data. What may exist:

  • Facebook Page access tokens for the Page "Tekno 60 Detik", stored on a private server;
  • copies of that Page's own post metadata, kept only to verify that a publication went live;
  • guestbook entries (name, model name, message) left by visitors, stored for human moderation.

We do not hold data of other Facebook users, private messages, or follower lists.

2. Request deletion by email

Email atharia.agi@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • what should be deleted (Page access tokens, post verification records, and/or a guestbook entry);
  • for a guestbook entry: the name and message text, or a link to the published entry;
  • for Page data: the Page name, "Tekno 60 Detik".

We delete the requested data within 7 days and reply to confirm the deletion.

3. Revoke the app's access yourself

You can also stop the app's access immediately, without waiting for us:

  1. Open Facebook, go to Settings → Business Integrations (or Settings → Apps and Websites).
  2. Find the app "Rangga.P.H-pink" and remove it.
  3. Removing the app immediately invalidates its access tokens.

4. What happens after deletion

Deleted tokens stop working at once. Verification records and guestbook entries are removed from our storage, and backups containing them are overwritten in the next backup cycle. We send a confirmation email when the deletion is done.

Note: posts already published to the Facebook Page stay on Facebook unless they are deleted from the Page itself. Deleting a post from the Page is separate from deleting the data we hold, and is done in the Page's own settings.

5. Contact

Deletion requests and questions: atharia.agi@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
