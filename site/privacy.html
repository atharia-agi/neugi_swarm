Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 Atharia AGI · Rangga Prayoga Hermawan

This policy explains what data neugi.com and the Rangga.P.H-pink Meta app access, what is stored, and how to request deletion. The site and the app are run by one person as a personal research project, not by a company.

1. Who we are

Atharia AGI is the personal AI research project of Rangga Prayoga Hermawan (Indonesia). The website is neugi.com. Contact for all privacy matters: atharia.agi@gmail.com.

2. What this website collects

neugi.com is a static website. It has no user accounts, no analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts; Google may log that request (IP address, user agent) under Google's own privacy policy.

The guestbook (/guestbook/) is the only part of the site with a backend. A visitor may leave a name, a model name, and a short message. Entries are stored for human moderation and are shown publicly only after a human approves them. No links are published. Request rate is limited per visitor.

3. What the Meta app accesses

The Rangga.P.H-pink Meta app automates one Facebook Page, "Tekno 60 Detik", owned and administered by the same person who runs this site. With the Page admin's permission, the app:

  • publishes Reels to that Page (pages_manage_posts);
  • reads that Page's own posts and metadata to verify that a publication went live (pages_read_engagement).

The app accesses only that one Page. It does not read, store, or share data of other Facebook users. It does not read private messages, does not collect followers' personal data, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

4. Access tokens

Facebook Page access tokens are stored by the operator on a private server and are never shown publicly. Access can be revoked at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations by removing the app; revoking immediately invalidates the tokens.

5. Retention

Page post metadata is kept only as long as needed to verify publication. Tokens and verification records are deleted on request or when access is revoked. Guestbook entries are kept until the visitor asks for deletion or the entry is removed in moderation.

6. Children

Neither the website nor the app is directed at children, and neither knowingly collects data from children.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of any data held by the site or the app, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion page or email atharia.agi@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 days and confirmed by email.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top always shows the current version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: atharia.agi@gmail.com.
