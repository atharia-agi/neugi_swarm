Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

1. Privacy Model

NEUGI is designed for local-first operation. By default, runtime state, memory, and configuration are stored in your local NEUGI directory and infrastructure under your control.

2. Data NEUGI Stores Locally

  • Configuration (for example provider, model, and endpoint settings)
  • Session and memory data (including local SQLite stores)
  • Operational logs and governance/audit records

3. External Provider Data Flow

When you configure third-party providers, prompts and related content are sent directly to those providers according to your configuration and their policies. NEUGI maintainers do not proxy that traffic as a hosted relay service.

4. Credentials and Secrets

You are responsible for securing API keys, tokens, and environment variables in your deployment. Use least-privilege credentials and rotate secrets regularly.

5. Telemetry and Analytics

NEUGI does not require centralized telemetry to operate. If you enable external monitoring integrations in your own environment, data handling follows the tools and services you choose.

6. Security Disclaimer

No system can guarantee perfect security. You remain responsible for host hardening, network controls, access management, backup strategy, and incident response.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You control retention, cleanup, and deletion in your own runtime. Review configured memory lifecycles, logs, and backup policies to align with your compliance needs.

8. International and Regulatory Use

If your deployment processes personal or regulated data, you are responsible for compliance with applicable frameworks (for example GDPR, PDPA, HIPAA, or industry-specific controls).

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as NEUGI evolves. Continued use after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
